Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Checkpoint Clipped is a private tool used by one person, its owner, to publish videos on the owner's own channel. It has no other users and collects no information from visitors to this page.

Access it requests

With the owner's permission, the app asks Google for access to the owner's own YouTube channel in order to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read the channel's own video list (youtube.readonly).

How it is used and stored

The access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. They are used only to upload and schedule videos on the owner's channel and to check what the owner has already posted. Nothing is sent to any server operated by us, and no data from YouTube is sold, shared, transferred to third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
